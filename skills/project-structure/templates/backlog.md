# Backlog

**Lifecycle:** Tracked standing agenda of unfinished work only. Placement is neither permission nor strict order.

## How to maintain this backlog

- **Current focus:** selected work. **Next:** ready, not selected. **Blocked:** waiting on named prerequisites. **Later:** concrete, deferred. **Ideas:** needs shaping.
- `[human]` marks work needing substantial human judgment. It is not blocked for that reason alone.
- Link dependencies both ways with **Blocked by** and **Unblocks**. Unblocking does not select an item.
- Before removing a finished item, update its dependents and preserve useful outcomes in their owning docs.
- Larger items link to `plans/<item>.md`.
